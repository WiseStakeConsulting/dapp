How to put in

Mint during the grand opening. The $5 mint fee goes to the studio treasury, which funds the protocol's legal work - and the studio moves a share of it into this pot, in public transactions anyone can audit. Every supporter who mints is funding the lawyer too. That is the whole idea: the community builds its own protections.

Or contribute directly. Send WISE or ETH straight to the pot address above from any wallet. Direct contributions skip the middle step and land in the pot itself, publicly, forever.

The honest fine print

Contributions are not investments: they buy no tokens, carry no return, and give no claim on Wise Stake Consulting LLC. Funds are spent at WSC's discretion on legal and compliance costs. Contributions are not tax-deductible. The pot is a dedicated Legal Fund wallet, separate from the WSC operating treasury.
