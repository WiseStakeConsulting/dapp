What you hold

Your live, earning positions - Signature Stakes (Wise One) and vault receipts (wtnUSDC / wsnUSDC). These are what back every yield-bearing NFT and bundle.

Only Signature Stakes appear here - they are the transferable, NFT form of a WISE stake. A stake created with "I prefer this stake to be transferable" switched off stays locked to its wallet: it still earns and scrapes, but it can never be bundled, collateralized, or sold. Check for the gold YIELD tag before counting a stake toward Wise Stake services.

Bundles - born as NFTs

A bundle combines up to two of three things: a Signature Stake, a vault position, or your minted NFT as the rider. In this dapp the bundle IS an NFT - which is what makes it tradable on the Marketplace tab with zero handshakes.

Start a stake on this rail

Read this before you open Wise One - the third one is the one people miss.

  1. Set your staking amount and duration. Arriving through a rail's door shows the 10% referral bonus automatically.
  2. On a custom rail not in the list? Use Wise One's "I want to use team referral address" selector and paste the address from the Custom field above.
  3. Turn ON "I prefer this stake to be transferable." That one switch makes your stake a Signature Stake - the transferable NFT that Wise Stake bundles, collateral, and the marketplace are built on. It is OFF by default; leaving it off locks the stake to your wallet forever.

WISE rule: a rail earns referral interest only at Critical Mass - $10,000 USD of referred stakes, valued on each stake's creation day. Stakes referred before that never earn retroactively.

Marketplace - atomic, in WISE

Seller lists, buyer pays WISE, the contract swaps ownership and splits the fee in one transaction. 2% to the WSC treasury, 98% to the seller. No trusting anybody - the contract does the moving.

The marketplace contract is not deployed yet. Deploy it from Remix with the guide in the repo (DEPLOY.md), then paste its address into js/config.js. Steps take about 10 minutes.
