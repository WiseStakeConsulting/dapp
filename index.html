Start a stake on this rail

Read this before you open Wise One - the second one is the one people miss.

  1. Set your staking amount and duration. Arriving through a rail's door shows the 10% referral bonus automatically. Leave the team referral address field blank - the rail you picked above already carries the credit.
  2. Turn ON "I prefer this stake to be transferable." That one switch makes your stake a Signature Stake - the transferable NFT that bundles, collateral, and the marketplace are built on. It is OFF by default; leaving it off locks the stake to your wallet forever.

WISE rule: a rail earns referral interest only at Critical Mass - $10,000 USD of referred stakes, valued on each stake's creation day. Stakes referred before that never earn retroactively.

Coming Soon - holders who might sell

A holder can signal that a token they own might be for sale someday. One transaction, no price, no commitment - the registry contract has no price field and no payment path. Withdraw your signal any time.

Private sales are exactly that - private. Wise Stake does not arrange, broker, or guarantee them, and never holds anyone's money. If you and another person agree to a sale, you settle it between yourselves: the seller transfers the token, the buyer pays separately, and anything a seller says about their token is the seller speaking for their own asset, not for us. Never pay first unless you fully trust the other side - there is no undo on a wallet-to-wallet transfer. Atomic purchases on the Marketplace open after legal review.
