Copyright & DMCA Policy

How rights holders reach us, and what we do about it.

Designated agent

Wise Stake Consulting LLC, Attn: Copyright Agent · legal@wisestake.consulting

Filing a notice

If your copyrighted work was minted here without permission, email our agent with:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed.
  2. The token's collection address and token ID on this dapp (the 0x... contract and number).
  3. Your name, address, phone, and email.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What we can do, honestly

The minting contract is permissionless: it runs on Ethereum, and no one - including us - can selectively block one wallet from it. Our remedies are real but limited: we can pause all minting network-wide by resetting the mint price (and will, on a valid notice, while the parties work it out), and we can remove references to a token from any surface this site controls. The record of a mint lives on the public blockchain regardless; only the courts can order otherwise.

Repeat infringers

We keep notices on file. Where a wallet is subject to repeated valid notices, we will exercise our pause and remove all references to that wallet's tokens from site surfaces, and document our response. Because minting is permissionless, account termination in the classic sense is not technically available to us - the pause and the delist are.

Counter-notification

If your mint was reported and you believe the report was mistaken, email the agent with the token details, your contact information, consent to jurisdiction of a federal court in the Southern District of New York, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was reported in error. We will pass your counter-notice to the reporter and restore our references if the matter resolves in your favor or no court action is filed within 10 business days.

Misuse warning

Knowingly filing a false notice or counter-notice may make you liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees, under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).
